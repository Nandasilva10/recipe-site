BRIGADEIROS

Soft, fudgy chocolate bites rolled in sprinkles. Brazil's favorite party sweet, made with just four things!

  • Makes about 20
  • 25 min cooking
  • 1 hr cooling

Ingredients!

How to make!

  1. MixAdd the condensed milk, cocoa, and butter to a nonstick saucepan. Stir until smooth.
  2. CookHeat on medium-low, stirring constantly with a spatula, for 10 to 12 minutes. It is ready when you drag the spatula across the bottom and the trail takes 2 to 3 seconds to close.
  3. CoolPour onto a buttered plate. Let it cool for about 1 hour, until firm enough to scoop.
  4. RollButter your hands. Scoop a teaspoon of the mixture and roll it into a ball.
  5. CoatRoll each ball in sprinkles until covered, then set it in a paper cup.

Some tips from fellow bakers!

  • Keep stirring the whole time. The mixture burns quickly on the bottom of the pan.
  • If it's too soft to roll, chill it for 15 minutes.
  • Store in a covered container at room temperature for up to 3 days, or in the fridge for up to a week.

Try other coatings: shredded coconut, chopped nuts, or colorful sprinkles.
